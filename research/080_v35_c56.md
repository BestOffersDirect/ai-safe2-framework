# Consequence-aware monitoring coverage | C56

**Status** Source-derived design analysis, v3.5. The requirements below define the v3.5 framework. Independent effectiveness verification remains deployment-specific.

## Question and source

October 3-9 security brief, finding B06; primary source https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07256v1. Retrospective disposition dated November 23, 2026.

The source record preserves historical proposals, corrections and limitations. Study percentages and analyst scores require the original study denominator and deployment context before use as prevalence or effectiveness claims. The reviewed source inventory is in the [expansion review](../docs/FRAMEWORK-EVIDENCE-EXPANSION.md).

## Placement and alternatives

A separate coverage control increases visibility but duplicates M4.4 and E5.1. Add explicit clauses plus the evaluator work package instead of a new threat category. Explicit selector and auditor metrics expose cost and safety trade-offs. Held-out evaluation and stratification add cost and cannot guarantee unseen-path coverage.

The primary owner is **M4.4 Adversarial Behavior Detection Pipeline**. Its child clauses preserve this distinct failure mechanism. The competing design is a separate control or deployment profile. Separation is justified when ownership, applicability, enforcement or acceptance cannot be represented by this parent. Absorption requires this explicit detail, a linked acceptance case and a migration record.

### OBS-2026-OCT-03 Selective trace auditing

**Reported facts in the supplied review** The study reports detection of 72.8% with selective auditing versus 81.5% with full auditing, with lower token use. Detection is not prevention.

**Source-assigned evidence** E3 in supplied brief; rating not independently reassessed

**Limits** Benchmark-specific and probabilistic; simulated/replayed multi-agent cases are not live operational efficacy.

## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **M4.4.R01** Declare the semantic-audit selection policy, mandatory inspection classes, exclusions, resource limits and behavior when evidence or reviewers are unavailable; selection cannot bypass mandatory final-effect authorization.
- **M4.4.R02** Measure selector misses, auditor misses, false blocks, unknowns, useful completion and latency separately by consequence class, runtime and backend, using paired and held-out cases.
- **M4.4.R03** Evaluate adversarial evasion and overload of the selector; material coverage degradation requires an explicit authority restriction or approved exception and retest.

## Adversarial validation and contrary evidence

Use the versioned V01-V10 specifications in challenges/003-evidence-boundary/cases.json. Record independent effects, paired legitimate work, unknown observations and recovery. Execution is tracked in #448/#449 and applicable component issues; this record does not claim a passed runtime test.

Include a legitimate-use control case, denied or unsafe conditions, missing evidence, unavailable dependencies, and the observed recovery outcome where applicable. Measure useful completion and false refusals alongside prevented effects. An existing implementation that demonstrably satisfies the same objective is contrary evidence to adding duplicate machinery. Preserve that result and revise the placement without deleting the underlying failure case.

## Evidence and interpretation

Record actor/origin, entry surface, permitted authority, persistence, affected resource, effect, enforcement point and recovery. Separate disclosed vulnerability, laboratory demonstration, observed exploitation, and incident attribution. Source publication, event and fix dates retain distinct meanings. Deterministic decisions require specified inputs and policy; they do not establish a general safety guarantee.

## Failure and recovery

The deployment owner records partial and unknown outcomes, contains the affected authority where warranted, preserves minimal reconstruction evidence, and revalidates after correction. Quantitative thresholds need declared units, population, uncertainty and policy ownership. Human-state or legal conclusions require their own validation and applicability review.
