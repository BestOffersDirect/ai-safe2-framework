## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **CP.13.R08** Treat evidence content as untrusted through display, preview and export; use inert rendering or a demonstrably isolated active-content boundary, constrained links and explicit parser failure handling.
- **CP.13.R09** Bind source collection records, retained content and exported artifacts with versioned transformations and completeness indicators; detect suppression, substitution and truncated exports.
- **CP.13.R10** Provide an independent verification path and retest after renderer/exporter changes; record disagreement between stored, displayed and exported evidence.
