## Requirements and implementation consequences

- **E5.4.R01** Record origin, trust, observed consequence, validity and permitted use for generated red-team artifacts and persistent strategy memory; quarantine unverified instructions.
- **E5.4.R02** Separate development feedback from held-out assurance; review, version, authorize and support rollback of changes promoted into production monitoring or policy.
- **E5.4.R03** Test poisoned strategy memory, benchmark leakage, malicious rule proposals and restoration; an artifact or its generating agent cannot authorize its own promotion.
